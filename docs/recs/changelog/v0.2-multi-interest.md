# v0.2 Multi-interest, diversity and cold start

Date: 2026-10-20 · Status: superseded by v0.3

## Changes
- **Multiple interests:** saved items are grouped with spherical k-means (up to 3 groups, deterministic seeding). Each group is one "interest" with its own vector and weight. An item's relevance is its best match across interests. This is a small version of the idea behind Pinterest's PinnerSage.
- **Diversity re-ranking (MMR):** items are picked one at a time, trading relevance (70%) against similarity to what is already chosen (30%).
- **Exploration:** 10% of slots are random picks from the unranked tail, labelled "Something different".
- **Cold start for new uploads:** a freshness boost that fades over about 3 days, so an upload with zero views gets shown.
- **Hidden-item penalty:** candidates very similar to something you hid are pushed down.
- **Taste panel** shows each interest with its top saved pins.

## Measured
Four personas × 30 simulated users, 5 rounds, 240-item catalog. Basic features. "Avg precision" is the mean over rounds of the share of the top 10 the user would like.

| Persona | v0.1 avg precision | v0.2 avg precision | v0.1 coverage | v0.2 coverage |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Art only | 68% | 58% | 80% | 90% |
| Music + Games | 69% | 78% | 57% | 82% |
| Art + Music + Writing | 84% | 91% | 64% | 77% |
| Pixel look (any category) | 77% | 57% | 97% | 87% |

Coverage = share of the user's interests that show up in the final feed (at least 2 items each).

- **Wins** for users with several interests: up to +9 points of precision, +25 points of coverage, and feeds about half as redundant (0.24 vs 0.47).
- **Loses** for single-interest users: down 10 to 20 points.

## Investigation
Turning each part off one at a time showed the cluster step itself was the cause. With only a few saves, k-means splits one interest into 3 noisy sub-clusters (the taste panel showed three saved "games" pins as three separate 33% interests). The pooled average is more stable than any one sub-cluster. MMR on its own actually helped every persona, so the diversity part is not at fault.

A first attempt to fix it by merging similar clusters and loosening diversity for narrow tastes made no measurable difference, and was removed.
